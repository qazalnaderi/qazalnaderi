## Selected Research & Projects

### [Adaptive Fact-Checker Placement](https://github.com/qazalnaderi/adaptive-fact-checker-placement)

Paper-aligned simulation framework for adaptive, boundary-aware fact-checker allocation across small-world, scale-free, and random social networks.

Associated publication:

[Adaptive Boundary-Aware Fact-Checker Placement for Misinformation Suppression in Social Networks](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16104740)

### [Steel Surface Defect Detection](https://github.com/qazalnaderi/steel-surface-defect-detection)

Industrial image-classification system comparing custom CNNs and ResNet18 transfer learning, with Grad-CAM interpretability and a desktop inference interface.
